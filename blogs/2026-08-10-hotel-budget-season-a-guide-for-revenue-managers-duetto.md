Hotel budget season is the annual planning period — typically Q3 into early Q4 — when hotels set next year's revenue targets and decide where commercial investment goes. For revenue managers, it's the one structured window each year to secure buy-in from finance, ownership, and the rest of the C-suite for the tools and strategy shifts they need. Budget season has a way of arriving faster than anyone plans for.
